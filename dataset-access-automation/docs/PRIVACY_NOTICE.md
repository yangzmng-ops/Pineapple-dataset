# Pineapple Dataset Application Privacy Notice

Version: 2026-10-11-v1

## Information collected and purpose

We collect the information submitted in the application form, including your name, institution or organization, verified Google Account email address, intended use, project description, use declarations, and acknowledgment of the access terms. We also keep the submission time and records needed to process the application, including the access decision, permission and notification status, and processing errors.

This information is used only for application review, access management, and related notifications. The Google Account email address is used to grant access and contact you about your application.

## Handling and access

Applications and processing records are handled using Google Forms and Google Sheets. Dataset permissions are managed through Google Drive, and access-related email is sent through Google's mail service.

Application information is not made public. Access to application records is limited to the dataset owner and authorized administrators handling these purposes.

## Retention and requests

Application information is retained for as long as needed for application review, access management, and related notifications.

You may email yangzmng@gmail.com to request correction or deletion of your application information.
